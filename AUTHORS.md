# Authors and Contributions

HEOFS (Hybrid Extreme Ozone Forecasting System) was developed through the integration of Temporal Fusion Transformer (TFT) models and Artificial Neural Networks (ANNs).

### Dr. Román D. Mondragón-Rodríguez
**Affiliation:** Universidad Autónoma Metropolitana, Iztapalapa Unit (UAM-I).

**Contributions:** Investigation (Lead), Methodology, Formal Analysis, Validation, Conceptualization, Supervision, Writing – Original Draft, Writing – Review & Editing, Data Curation. Contributed to the methodology and development of the Artificial Neural Network components.  Developed the hybrid forecasting framework.

### Dr. Carlos Gay-García
**Affiliation:** Instituto de Ciencias de la Atmósfera y Cambio Climático, Universidad Nacional Autónoma de México (ICAyCC-UNAM)

**Contributions:** Investigation (Lead), Supervision, Project Administration, Conceptualization.

### Joshua I. Ruíz-Rivera
**Affiliation:** Instituto de Ciencias de la Atmósfera y Cambio Climático, Universidad Nacional Autónoma de México (ICAyCC-UNAM)

**Contributions:** Writing – Review & Editing, Writing – Original Draft, Software, Conceptualization, Investigation, Data Curation, Visualization, Resources. Developed and implemented the Temporal Fusion Transformer models, including the ozone and atmospheric precursor forecasting components. Integrated the TFT and ANN components.
